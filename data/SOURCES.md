## Notes per domain

### climate

- **What it is:** NOAA/NCEI 1/4-degree Daily Optimum Interpolation SST v2.1 (AVHRR-only, 'Final'), NOAA Level 4 analysis for 2024-09-01. Optimum interpolation of AVHRR satellite SST (MetOp-A/B) blended with ship, buoy and Argo in-situ SST; sea-ice concentration converted to proxy SST at the ice margin. Global 0.25-degree grid (1440 x 720, lat -90..90, lon 0..360), one time step, one zlev. Variables (all Celsius except ice in %): sst (daily SST), anom (daily SST anomaly), err (estimated error std-dev), ice (sea-ice concentration). Product record from 1981-09-01 to present; v2.1 replaced v2 in April 2020.
- **Format:** NetCDF-4 (CF-1.6, ACDD-1.3)
- **Processing:** None (complete original file).
- **Licence evidence:** data.gov catalog record for this dataset (publisher NOAA NCEI) lists License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. NCEI ISO record (https://data.noaa.gov/waf/NOAA/NESDIS/ncei/aim/iso/xml/C01606.xml) adds only no-warranty distribution/use liability disclaimers and points to the CDR use agreement.
- **DOI:** 10.25921/RE9P-PT57

### astronomy

- **What it is:** TESS SPOC 2-minute-cadence light curve for TIC 261136679 (TESS mag 5.1, Teff ~5990 K, R ~1.15 Rsun; this TIC ID is the bright star pi Mensae / HD 39091 - cross-ID not stated in the file itself), Sector 1 (2018-07-25 to 2018-08-22), Camera 4 / CCD 2, SPOC pipeline spoc-5.0.10. LIGHTCURVE table (20,076 cadences): TIME, SAP_FLUX, PDCSAP_FLUX (+ errors), SAP_BKG, QUALITY, PSF/MOM centroids, POS_CORR; APERTURE HDU holds the 11x21 pixel aperture mask. Instrument: TESS Photometer (4 wide-field cameras).
- **Format:** FITS (primary + LIGHTCURVE binary table + APERTURE image)
- **Processing:** None (complete original file).
- **Licence evidence:** Most data hosted at MAST are in the public domain (see: open data), and therefore do not have restrictions on use. There are, however, expectations ... that researchers acknowledge the originating mission and (usually) STScI.
- **DOI:** 10.17909/t9-nmc8-f686

### particle-physics

- **What it is:** CMS Monte Carlo simulation (not collision data) of Standard Model Higgs (m=125 GeV) -> ZZ -> 4 leptons in 8 TeV pp collisions, derived from /SMHiggsToZZTo4L_M-125_8TeV-powheg15-JHUgenV3-pythia6/Summer12_DR53X-PU_S10_START53_V19-v1/AODSIM (CERN Open Data record 9356, CMS 2012 conditions). Reduced to a flat NanoAOD-like ntuple with 299,973 events; 32 branches: run/luminosityBlock/event, primary vertex (PV_npvs, PV_x/y/z), muons and electrons (pt, eta, phi, mass, charge, isolation, dxy, dz + errors), MET_pt/phi. Intended for the H->4l outreach analysis (record 12360).
- **Format:** ROOT (TTree 'Events', reduced NanoAOD)
- **Processing:** None (complete original file; adler32 1dafc1d7 matches the record).
- **Licence evidence:** Record 12361 metadata: license CC0-1.0. Terms of use: 'All metadata and datasets in the CERN Open Data Portal are made available under the terms of the CC0 waiver.'
- **DOI:** 10.7483/OPENDATA.CMS.8FLU.UIQJ

### genomics

- **What it is:** Genome Aggregation Database (gnomAD) v2.1.1 exome sites VCF for chromosome 21, GRCh37. Aggregate allele-frequency summary statistics from 125,748 exomes (no individual genotypes or sample IDs). Variants called with GATK HaplotypeCaller, QC'd with Hail; random-forest filter (FILTER: PASS/RF/AC0/InbreedingCoeff). INFO (~869 fields): AC/AN/AF overall and by ancestry group and sex, nhomalt, subset frequencies (non_cancer, non_neuro, non_topmed, controls), site QC metrics (QD, MQ, FS, SOR, DP...), and Ensembl VEP consequence annotations (vep field incl. LoFTEE).
- **Format:** VCF 4.2, BGZF-compressed (tabix-indexable)
- **Processing:** TRIMMED: first 10 MiB fetched by HTTP Range from the 684 MB original, decompressed, complete 899-line header kept plus the first 3,323 complete records (21:9,825,785-10,942,691), last partial record dropped, re-compressed with bgzip (validated with tabix). sha256 is of the trimmed file, not the original.
- **Licence evidence:** The primary data from the gnomAD exomes and genomes are available free of restrictions under the Creative Commons Zero Public Domain Dedication. (Verified from the page source: https://github.com/broadinstitute/gnomad-browser/blob/main/browser/about/policies/terms.md - the live page is JavaScript-rendered.) Exception noted on the same page: SpliceAI annotations are CC BY-NC 4.0 - this v2.1.1 file contains no SpliceAI/CADD/Pangolin/PrimateAI fields.

### bioimaging

- **What it is:** BBBC039v1: DNA channel (Hoechst stain) of U2OS cells from a high-throughput chemical screen (sample of the BBBC022 Cell Painting screen, 200 bioactive compounds, one field of view per compound), fluorescence microscopy. 696 x 520 px, 16-bit greyscale TIFF written by MetaMorph 3.1.0.77 (plate well/site encoded in filename, e.g. A02 site 1; barcode BR00021381 in ImageDescription); MetaMorph tags give 0.645 um/pixel, channel name DAPI, 25 ms exposure, 2x2 binning, acquisition 2010-03-05. Dataset purpose: nucleus segmentation benchmark (~23,000 annotated nuclei; masks not included here).
- **Format:** TIFF (16-bit greyscale, MetaMorph)
- **Processing:** Single member extracted (HTTP Range read of the zip central directory + member, CRC verified) from images.zip (77.9 MB, 200 images); file bytes are unmodified.
- **Licence evidence:** Copyright: CC0. To the extent possible under law, the various contributors of the imagesets have waived all copyright and related or neighboring rights to BBBC039v1.

### public-sector

- **What it is:** UK Department for Transport road traffic statistics: Annual Average Daily Flow (AADF) by count point for the Wales region (region_id 4, ONS W92000004), years 2000-2025, 37,338 rows x 31 columns. Columns: count_point_id, year, region/local authority ids and names, road_name, road_type (major/minor), start/end junction, easting/northing, latitude/longitude, link length (km/miles), estimation_method ('Counted' vs 'Estimated') and detail, and AADF by vehicle class (pedal_cycles, two-wheeled motor vehicles, cars_and_taxis, buses_and_coaches, lgvs, 6 HGV axle classes, all_hgvs, all_motor_vehicles). DfT note: regional/national figures are accredited official statistics; link-level estimates are less robust.
- **Format:** CSV (comma-separated, quoted header, UTF-8)
- **Processing:** None (complete original file).
- **Licence evidence:** roadtraffic.dft.gov.uk footer (downloads and terms pages): 'Open Government Licence v3.0, except where otherwise stated', Crown copyright. data.gov.uk record 'GB Road Traffic Counts' (https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/208c0e7b-353f-4e2d-8b7a-1a7118467acc/gb-road-traffic-counts), publisher Department for Transport: Licence 'UK Open Government Licence (OGL)'.

### Substitutions and caveats

- **genomics:** 1000 Genomes / IGSR was not used. Its pages state only that data are released under the Fort Lauderdale Agreement and the EMBL-EBI terms of use, and that rights 'vary' — no explicit licence permitting commercial use. Substituted gnomAD v2.1.1 (explicit CC0; aggregate frequencies, no per-individual genotypes). gnomAD v3.1.2/v4.1 VCFs were rejected because they embed SpliceAI (CC BY-NC) and CADD scores.
- **bioimaging:** OME sample OME-TIFF `tubhiswt-3D` (CC-BY 4.0) was downloaded and then discarded because its embedded OME-XML contains an Experimenter record with a named individual's email address. BBBC017-derived OME-TIFFs are CC BY-NC-SA (rejected). BBBC039 (CC0) used instead; note its TIFF ImageDescription still contains an experiment-set label that is a researcher's name (a published author of the source screen), no contact details.
- **climate:** NCEI's own metadata landing page/geoportal returned errors on retrieval day; the CC0 licence was confirmed from the data.gov catalog record (harvested from NCEI) and NCEI's ISO XML on data.noaa.gov. The data.gov record oddly shows 'Access Level: non-public', which contradicts the open file server; treat as a harvest artefact.
